This is where I keep academic material from courses I took or teach, including my own notes, write-ups, and derivations, as well as collected course materials and past exams.

## Why This Exists

I'm a Mathematics Teaching Assistant at Özyeğin University, covering Single-Variable Calculus, Multivariable Calculus, Differential Equations, and Probability and Statistics. Explaining the same theorem forty times to forty different people is the fastest way to find out which parts of your understanding are load-bearing. The rest of what's here comes from courses I took or research I got pulled into.

So these are written for the student who asks "but why" four times in a row. I wonder who that student was.

They're also here because notes that live only on my laptop have a way of not existing.
